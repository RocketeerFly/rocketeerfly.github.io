List Picker

Shuffle and randomize any list of items

Quick Start

  1. Enter your items in the text box, one item per line.
  2. Tap Shuffle to randomize the order.
  3. The first item in the result is highlighted as the "winner."

Use Cases

  • Raffle / Giveaway — enter participant names and shuffle to pick a winner.
  • Team Assignment — shuffle students or team members into random groups.
  • Decision Making — enter your options and let randomness decide.
  • Presentation Order — randomize the speaking order for a meeting.
Tip: Tap "Again" to re-shuffle without re-entering items. Use "Copy Result" to share the randomized order.
