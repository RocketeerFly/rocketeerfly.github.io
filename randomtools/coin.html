Heads or tails — tap to flip

Quick Start

  1. Tap the coin or press the Flip Coin button.
  2. The coin spins in 3D and lands on Heads or Tails.
  3. View your stats and flip history below.

Features

  • Stats Tracker — see total heads, tails, and your current streak.
  • Ratio Bar — visual heads/tails ratio updates in real time.
  • History — your recent flips are saved locally.
  • Keyboard — press Space or Enter to flip.
Tip: Perfect for quick decisions, game starts, or settling friendly disputes.
