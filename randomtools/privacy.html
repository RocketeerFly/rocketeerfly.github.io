Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 15, 2025

Random Tools ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Random Tools website. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our service.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. Random Tools is a fully client-side application. All processing happens in your browser, and no data is ever sent to any server.

Local Storage

We use your browser's localStorage to save:

  • Your tool preferences (e.g., dice count, dice color, number range)
  • Recent result history (e.g., last 20 generated numbers or dice rolls)
  • Sound preference (on/off)

This data is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to us or any third party. You can clear this data at any time by clearing your browser's site data or using the "Clear" buttons within the tools.

Cookies

Random Tools does not use cookies of any kind — no tracking cookies, no session cookies, no advertising cookies.

Analytics & Tracking

We do not use any analytics services. There is no Google Analytics, no Facebook Pixel, no tracking scripts of any kind. We have no visibility into who uses this site, how often, or from where.

Third-Party Services

Random Tools does not load any third-party scripts, fonts, or resources. The entire application is self-contained. There are no external API calls, CDN dependencies, or embedded content from other services.

Advertising

Random Tools is completely ad-free. There are no advertisements, sponsored content, or affiliate links.

Data Security

Since we don't collect, store, or transmit any personal data, there is no personal data at risk. Your randomization results and preferences remain exclusively on your device.

Children's Privacy

Random Tools does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13. Since no data is collected at all, this service is safe for users of all ages.

GDPR & CCPA Compliance

Because Random Tools collects no personal data, processes no personal data, and stores no personal data on our servers, the service is inherently compliant with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). There is nothing to request, export, or delete on our end.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Your continued use of the service after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
