Dice Roller

Roll up to 5 dice with 3D animation

Quick Start

  1. Choose how many dice to roll (1–5) using the chips below.
  2. Pick a dice color from the color palette.
  3. Tap Roll Dice or press Space/Enter to roll.

Features

  • 3D Animation — dice tumble with realistic CSS 3D transforms.
  • Sound Effects — toggle the speaker icon in the top-right to mute/unmute.
  • Total & Breakdown — see the sum and individual values after each roll.
  • History — all rolls are saved so you can review past results.
Tip: Great for board games, tabletop RPGs, or settling decisions with a dice roll.
