Random Number

Generate a random number in your range

Quick Start

  1. Enter a minimum and maximum value (defaults to 0–100).
  2. Tap Generate to get a random number in that range.
  3. Use Copy to copy the result to your clipboard.

Features

  • No Repeats — toggle this on to avoid getting the same number twice in a row.
  • History — your recent results are saved and shown below. Tap any entry to copy it.
  • Keyboard — press Space or Enter to generate without clicking.
Tip: Your min/max settings are saved automatically and restored next time you visit.
