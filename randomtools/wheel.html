Spin the Wheel

Add names or items, spin, and pick a winner

Quick Start

  1. Type your entries in the text box below (one per line).
  2. Tap Spin to spin the wheel.
  3. The wheel slows down and stops on a random winner.

Features

  • Live Preview — the wheel updates instantly as you type.
  • Remove Winner — toggle to auto-remove the winner after each spin for elimination rounds.
  • Sound Effects — hear the ticker as the wheel slows. Toggle sound in the top-right.
  • History — past winners are saved below for reference.
  • Keyboard — press Space or Enter to spin (when not typing).
Tip: Great for classroom picks, raffle draws, team selections, or settling any group decision fairly.
